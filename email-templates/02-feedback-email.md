# Feedback Email — Post Coffee Chat Survey

**When sent:** After coordinator marks the coffee chat as completed  
**Status transition:** Chat Completed → Feedback Sent  
**Recipients:** Prospective student only

---

## Subject

```
How was your MSTI Coffee Chat? We'd love your feedback!
```

## Body

```
Hi [STUDENT_FIRST],

Thank you for connecting with [AMB_NAME] as part of the MSTI Coffee Chat Program! We hope the conversation gave you a better sense of the program and what to expect.

We'd love to hear about your experience. Your feedback helps us improve the program for future prospective students — it only takes 2–3 minutes.

[ Share your feedback → ]

Thank you, and we hope to see you in the MSTI program!

Best,
MSTI Admissions Ambassadors
```

---

## Variables

| Placeholder | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| `[STUDENT_FIRST]` | First name of the prospective student |
| `[AMB_NAME]` | Full name of the assigned ambassador |

---

## Notes

- The `[ Share your feedback → ]` line should be replaced with the actual feedback form link (Jotform or Google Form URL) before sending.
