# Intro Email — Connecting Student with Ambassador

**When sent:** After coordinator assigns an ambassador to a prospective student request  
**Status transition:** Pending Assignment → Intro Sent  
**Recipients:** Prospective student + Assigned ambassador (CC both)

---

## Subject

```
Prospective Student Connecting with MSTI Students and Alumni
```

## Body

```
Dear [STUDENT_FIRST] and [AMB_FIRST],

Thank you both for your interest in connecting with the MSTI network. Let me introduce you to each other!

• Meet [STUDENT_FIRST]: a prospective MSTI student eager to learn more about the program and alumni experiences.
  Email: [STUDENT_EMAIL]

• Meet [AMB_NAME]: [AMB_ROLE].
  Email: [AMB_EMAIL]
  LinkedIn: [AMB_LINKEDIN]

You're welcome to connect directly via email to arrange a call, virtual meeting, or even an in-person visit based on your preferences. Hope this leads to a meaningful and engaging conversation.

If you need any assistance, feel free to reply to this email. Enjoy the chat!

Best,
MSTI Admissions Ambassadors
```

---

## Variables

| Placeholder | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| `[STUDENT_FIRST]` | First name of the prospective student |
| `[STUDENT_EMAIL]` | Email address of the prospective student |
| `[AMB_FIRST]` | First name of the assigned ambassador |
| `[AMB_NAME]` | Full name of the assigned ambassador |
| `[AMB_EMAIL]` | Email address of the ambassador |
| `[AMB_LINKEDIN]` | LinkedIn URL of the ambassador |
| `[AMB_ROLE]` | `"an MSTI alumni happy to share insights and support future students"` (alumni) or `"an MSTI student ambassador happy to share insights and support future students"` (current student) |
